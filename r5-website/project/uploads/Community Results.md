# 06 — Results & Community

Standalone design brief for the **proof / community page**. Paste into
Claude Design with the R5 design-system output. Desktop (~1280) +
mobile (~390).

> Uses the **R5 brand design system**. Bilingual UA/EN. Apply the system;
> don't re-specify colour/type.

---

## Purpose & goal

Provide the **social proof** that turns interest into trust: this gym
gets people results, and it's a community worth belonging to. Mixes hard
proof (athlete achievements, competition results) with human proof
(member stories, the room's culture). Backs up the Coach page's claims
with *other people's* outcomes.

**Primary CTA:** *Book a free first session.*

---

## Desktop structure (top → bottom)

1. **Header.** Heavy headline (*Results / What R5 builds / Our people*),
   one framing line — real people, real progress, a room that competes
   together. Cream canvas; optional slim community photo band.

2. **Headline achievements strip.** The marquee wins, including the ones
   that prove the *coaching* (not just Dmytro's own): the mentored
   **Strongman world champion**, the **Milan 2018 Masters kettlebell**
   title, plus Dmytro's national #1. Stat/credential blocks; "earned, not
   gamified" treatment.

3. **Member testimonials.** The core. A set of quote blocks / cards —
   member photo (where cleared), name, a short honest quote about training
   at R5. Hand-made / marker energy. On desktop, a 2–3 column grid or a
   featured-quote-plus-grid layout.

4. **Transformation / progress stories.** A few deeper stories (a
   beginner who stuck with it, a masters athlete who got stronger, someone
   who trained around injury via the recovery approach). Optional
   before/after or "where they started → now." Keep honest and human, not
   infomercial.

5. **The community in pictures.** A photo strip of the room full,
   competitions, the whiteboard with scores, social moments. Reinforces
   belonging. Raw, real.

6. **Competition / board culture.** Tie back to the whiteboard ritual —
   athletes write scores, the room competes. Could embed recent
   leaderboard-style results (e.g. the kind of community Open standings the
   gym already posts). Marker/chalk character.

7. **Persistent CTA band.** "Want to be on the board?" → *Book a free
   first session.*

8. **Footer.** Global.

---

## Mobile adaptation (~390)

- **Achievements strip (2):** 2-up stat grid.
- **Testimonials (3):** single-column stacked cards or a swipe carousel;
  keep quotes short enough to read on one screen.
- **Stories (4):** full-width stacked; image-first.
- **Community photos (5):** swipe filmstrip.
- **Board culture (6):** if showing results/leaderboard, make it
  vertically scannable, not a wide table.
- Touch targets 44px+, CTA 56px.

---

## Content blocks needed (placeholders)

- Marquee achievements (the verified ones above are usable now).
- **Member testimonials — request real quotes + photos from Dmytro;**
  this page needs genuine voices, not placeholder lorem.
- 2–3 transformation/progress stories (with consent).
- Community + competition photos.
- Any recent community competition results the gym posts.

---

## Bilingual notes

- Testimonials will mostly be Ukrainian originally — present them in their
  original language with an English rendering available via the toggle;
  keep attribution (name) consistent across both.
- Achievement labels run longer in Ukrainian — design stat blocks to wrap.
