## Purpose & goal

This is the **trust engine** of the whole site. A visitor who lands here
should leave thinking "this person is the real deal, and he can get *me*
results." It carries the credibility the rest of the site leans on.

**Primary CTA:** *Book a free first session.*
**Secondary CTA:** *Train online with Dmytro* (this page legitimately
serves both audiences, so the online CTA earns a place here).

---

## Desktop structure (top → bottom)

1. **Header portrait + name.** Large, raw portrait (left or full-bleed
   band) with heavy name lockup: *Dmytro Pasichnyi* + role line
   (*Owner & Head Coach, R5*). One punch line beneath — the "still the
   fittest in his division" idea. Can use the dark treatment.

2. **The opening statement.** Two to three short paragraphs in his voice —
   plain, no hype — on how he coaches and what he believes (strength that
   lasts; training the nervous system, not just the muscles). Set in
   comfortable reading width on cream.

3. **Career timeline.** A vertical or horizontal timeline of the
   milestones above (2001 coaching → 2005 degree → 2012 Master of Sport /
   Hamburg → 2018 Milan athlete win → 2019 Marbella → 2026 national #1).
   Marker/chalk treatment fits well here. Keep it scannable.

4. **The method (HRV) — featured block.** A dedicated, visually distinct
   section explaining the recovery-science approach in plain language:
   what HRV is, why it matters, how he uses it. This is the
   differentiator; give it room. Could be a darker "manual page" panel.

5. **Credentials grid.** Honest, understated grid of certifications and
   titles (CrossFit L2, Gymnastics, HRV, Master of Sport, degree). Stat/
   credential-block component. Earned, not badge-spammy.

6. **Athletes he's built.** Short proof section — the Strongman world
   champ, the Milan medallist — as evidence he develops *others*. Links
   to Results & Community.

7. **Quote / philosophy pull.** One large hand-set quote in his voice.

8. **Dual CTA band.** Two clear paths: *Train with me in Dnipro → book a
   free session* and *Train with me online →*. Keep them visually
   balanced so each audience self-selects.

9. **Footer.** Global.

## Mobile adaptation (~390)

- **Portrait + name:** portrait full-width on top, name/role lockup below
  it; punch line as a single heavy line.
- **Timeline (3):** switch to a single vertical line, milestones stacked;
  keep dates prominent.
- **HRV block (4):** full-width panel; shorten to the essentials, keep the
  plain-language explanation.
- **Credentials grid (5):** 2-up grid, not a wide row.
- **Dual CTA (8):** stack the two CTAs vertically; **local/book is the
  top (primary) one**, online below.
- Touch targets 44px+, primary CTA 56px.
