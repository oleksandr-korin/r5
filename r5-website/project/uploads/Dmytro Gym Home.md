# 01 — Home

Standalone design brief for the **R5 homepage**. Paste into Claude Design
together with the R5 design-system output. Covers desktop (~1280) and
mobile (~390).

> Uses the **R5 brand design system** (raw plywood + black + warm amber,
> whiteboard energy, heavy Cyrillic-complete display face, light/cream
> canvas primary, dark hero). Don't re-specify colour or type — apply the
> system. Bilingual UA/EN; design every block in both.

---

## Purpose & goal

The homepage is the hook and the switchboard. In one scroll it must:
make a visitor *feel* the gym, establish in seconds that the coach is the
real deal, and route them to the right next step. **Primary goal: book a
free first session.** Secondary: send remote visitors to online coaching.

**Primary CTA:** *Book a free first session.*
**Secondary CTA:** *Train online with Dmytro.*

---

## Desktop structure (top → bottom)

1. **Hero (dark).** Full-bleed, heavy headline over a strong photo of the
   gym/coach in action. Headline carries the brand promise (e.g. *"Train
   like it matters."* / a line about strength that lasts). One short
   supporting sentence. Primary CTA button + a quiet secondary link
   (online coaching). This is the one dark section up top; the page warms
   to cream below. Heavy uppercase display type; marker-style underline
   accent permitted.

2. **The one-line proof bar.** A slim band of 3–4 credibility hits as
   stat/credential blocks: *#1 Masters 40+ in Ukraine · 25 years coaching
   · CrossFit L2 + Gymnastics · HRV-certified*. Earned, not gamified.

3. **Who we are (the box).** Two-column on desktop: short, plain-spoken
   paragraph on what R5 is (real working gym, serious training,
   community that competes) beside a raw interior photo. Quiet confidence,
   no hype.

4. **Why R5 is different — the three pillars.** Three cards in a row:
   *Proven & still competing* · *Trains your recovery, not just your
   reps (HRV)* · *Builds champions*. Each: heavy title, one or two lines,
   a marker/chalk accent. Cards carry R5 texture (defend in design
   system).

5. **What you can do here (programs teaser).** Compact grid of the in-gym
   offerings (group classes, personal training, beginner on-ramp) with a
   link to Training & Programs. Each as a textured card.

6. **Meet the coach (teaser).** Portrait + a few heavy lines on Dmytro +
   his headline credentials, linking to the full Coach page. This is the
   bridge to the trust engine.

7. **Proof / community strip.** A short row of athlete results or one
   strong testimonial quote, linking to Results & Community.

8. **Online coaching teaser.** A single band for the remote audience —
   "Not in Dnipro? Train with Dmytro's programming and HRV method
   online." Secondary CTA. Visually distinct (could borrow the dark
   treatment) so it doesn't compete with local CTAs above.

9. **Persistent CTA band.** "Ready to start?" → *Book a free first
   session.* Big warm-amber button. Reused global block.

10. **Footer.** Global.

## Mobile adaptation (~390)

- **Hero:** headline stays heavy but reflows to 2–3 lines; photo behind
  with a darken overlay so type holds contrast; **single full-width
  primary CTA**, secondary becomes a text link below it. Sticky CTA bar
  appears on scroll.
- **Proof bar:** wraps to a 2×2 grid of stat blocks, not a single row.
- **Two-column sections (3, 6):** stack to image-over-text; keep the photo
  first so the *feel* lands before the words.
- **Three pillars (4):** vertical stack of full-width cards; preserve heavy
  titles.
- **Programs teaser (5):** horizontal swipe carousel or stacked cards.
- **Order priority on mobile:** Hero → proof bar → pillars → coach teaser
  → programs → proof strip → online teaser → CTA. (Pull the coach teaser
  up slightly on mobile — trust matters faster on a small screen.)
- Touch targets 44px+, primary CTA 56px.

## Bilingual notes

- Headline must work heavy and uppercase in **both** Cyrillic and Latin —
  test the display face here first; this is the most type-critical block.
- Proof-bar labels run longer in Ukrainian — design the stat block to
  wrap label text without breaking the number.
