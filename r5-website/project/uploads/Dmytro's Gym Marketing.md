# 03 — The Gym (R5)

Standalone design brief for the **gym page**. Paste into Claude Design
with the R5 design-system output. Desktop (~1280) + mobile (~390).

> Uses the **R5 brand design system**. Bilingual UA/EN. Apply the system;
> don't re-specify colour/type.

---

## Purpose & goal

Answer the unspoken question every prospect has: *"What's it actually
like in there, and will I belong?"* This page sells the **space and the
culture** — the raw, warm, serious-but-welcoming working box — and
removes the "I'm not fit enough / I won't fit in" fear. This is the most
**photo-led** page on the site; the design system's plywood/whiteboard
DNA should be most literal here.

**Primary CTA:** *Book a free first session.*

---

## Desktop structure (top → bottom)

1. **Atmosphere hero.** Big, raw photo of the box — plywood, black
   ceiling, tungsten light, chalk, the whiteboard. Heavy short headline
   over it (e.g. *"This is the room."* / a line about the floor where the
   work happens). Can be dark. Let the photo carry it.

2. **What R5 is (and isn't).** A short, honest two-column statement:
   working gym, not boutique; serious training, real community; everyone
   from first-timers to competitors. Plain voice. Pair with a candid
   floor photo.

3. **The whiteboard ritual.** A signature section built around the daily
   WOD-on-the-board culture — athletes write their scores, the room
   competes. This is the brand's soul; design it with the most
   marker/chalk character on the site. Photo of the board + a few lines
   on why it matters.

4. **Photo gallery / the space.** A grid or filmstrip of the gym: rig,
   floor, equipment, light, people training. Raw and real, not staged
   stock. On desktop, an asymmetric masonry or 2–3 column grid.

5. **A typical class / what to expect.** Reassurance block: how a session
   runs (warm-up → strength → WOD → cool-down), scalability for any
   level, coached throughout. Reduces first-visit anxiety. Could borrow a
   "manual page" panel look.

6. **Practical info.** Location + map, hours, how to get there, parking,
   first-visit logistics ("what to bring"). Clean, functional block on
   cream.

7. **Persistent CTA band.** "Come see it for yourself" → *Book a free
   first session.*

8. **Footer.** Global.

---

## Mobile adaptation (~390)

- **Atmosphere hero:** full-bleed photo, darken overlay, heavy headline,
  single CTA.
- **Two-column (2):** stack image-first.
- **Whiteboard ritual (3):** full-width photo + text below; keep the
  marker character.
- **Gallery (4):** horizontal swipe filmstrip or a single-column stack of
  full-width photos (lazy-loaded). Avoid tiny thumbnails.
- **Map (6):** full-width embedded map with a tap-to-open-directions
  button; address and hours stacked below.
- Touch targets 44px+, primary CTA 56px.

---

## Content blocks needed (placeholders)

  **request from Dmytro; this page lives or dies on real photography.**
- "What R5 is" copy (UA/EN).
- Whiteboard-ritual copy.
- "What to expect in a class" copy.
- Address, map embed, hours, getting-there/parking, what-to-bring —
  *content doc supplies; use realistic stand-ins.*

---

## Bilingual notes

- Practical-info labels (hours, directions, what to bring) must be clear
  in both languages — these are high-intent, pre-visit details.
- Keep the whiteboard-ritual copy short so it holds its punch in both UA
  and EN.

## What to design (paste into Claude Design)

Design **"The Gym" page** for R5, a raw working CrossFit box in Dnipro,
desktop (~1280) and mobile (~390), in the R5 brand system. This is the
most photo-led, most literally on-brand page — plywood, black ceiling,
tungsten light, chalk, the whiteboard. Sections: atmosphere photo hero

staged-stock. Show Ukrainian and English. Mobile: full-bleed hero,
two-column sections stack image-first, gallery becomes a swipe filmstrip,
map full-width with tap-to-directions.
