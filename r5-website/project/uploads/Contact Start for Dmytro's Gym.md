# 07 — Start Here / Contact

Standalone design brief for the **conversion / contact page**. Paste into
Claude Design with the R5 design-system output. Desktop (~1280) +
mobile (~390).

> Uses the **R5 brand design system**. Bilingual UA/EN. Apply the system;
> don't re-specify colour/type.

---

## Purpose & goal

This is where intent becomes action. Make booking a **free first
session** effortless, and offer low-friction ways to reach the gym for
people who'd rather message than fill a form. Everything on this page
serves one job: **get the visitor to take the first step.**

**Primary CTA / goal:** book a free first session (form submit).
**Alternate paths:** Instagram DM, phone/WhatsApp, just show up.

---

## Desktop structure (top → bottom)

1. **Header.** Heavy, warm, direct headline (*Start here* / *Your first
   session is on us*). One line removing friction: free, coached, no
   experience needed, no pressure. Cream canvas, can open dark.

2. **The booking block (centrepiece).** A clean lead-capture form — name,
   contact (phone or email), preferred day/time or "first time / been
   before", optional note. Big warm-amber submit. Keep fields minimal —
   every extra field costs conversions. 44px+ fields, AA contrast on the
   amber CTA.

   Beside it (desktop two-column): a reassurance panel — what happens
   after you book (we'll reply via [channel], here's what your first visit
   looks like, what to bring). Kills uncertainty.

3. **Other ways to reach us.** Equal-weight quick options as cards/buttons:
   **Instagram DM** (`@rokytnyi5.crossbox` — the gym's live channel),
   **phone / WhatsApp / Telegram**, **email**. Meet Ukrainian users where
   they already are; many will prefer DM over a form.

4. **Find us.** Map embed + address, hours, getting there / parking. Same
   practical block style as the Gym page (reuse the pattern).

5. **Quick FAQ (optional).** 3–4 friction-killers: Do I need to be fit?
   What do I bring? Is the first session really free? Can I just turn up?
   Accordion.

6. **Online alternative (low emphasis).** One line for the remote
   audience: "Not in Dnipro? → Online coaching." Doesn't compete with the
   booking block.

7. **Footer.** Global.

---

## Mobile adaptation (~390)

- **Booking block (2):** form first, full-width, single column; the
  reassurance panel moves **below** the form (don't push the form down).
  Submit is a full-width 56px amber button.
- **Other ways to reach us (3):** stacked full-width tap targets — DM,
  call, message — each a one-tap action (`tel:`, deep links). On mobile
  these may convert *better* than the form; give them prominence.
- **Map (4):** full-width with tap-to-open-directions.
- **FAQ (5):** accordion.
- Sticky primary CTA optional here (the page *is* the CTA).

---

## Content blocks needed (placeholders)

- Form fields + post-submit reassurance copy (UA/EN).
- Contact channels — Instagram is real (`@rokytnyi5.crossbox`); **confirm
  phone / messaging / email with Dmytro.**
- Address, map embed, hours, directions/parking — *content doc supplies.*
- FAQ answers (free first session, what to bring, fitness level, drop-in).

---

## Bilingual notes

- Form labels, placeholders, and the post-submit/confirmation message must
  exist in both languages.
- Keep the headline's warmth intact in Ukrainian — don't let it become
  stiff/formal in translation.
- Ukrainian form labels run longer; ensure fields and buttons don't break.

---

## What to design (paste into Claude Design)

Design the **Start Here / Contact page** for R5, desktop (~1280) and
mobile (~390), in the R5 brand system. Single job: get the visitor to book
a free first session, with low-friction alternatives for people who prefer
to message. Sections: warm heavy header that removes friction (free,
coached, no experience needed) → centrepiece booking block — a minimal
lead-capture form (name, contact, first-time-or-not, optional note, big
warm-amber submit) beside a "what happens next / what to bring"
reassurance panel → equal-weight "other ways to reach us" cards (Instagram
DM @rokytnyi5.crossbox, phone/WhatsApp/Telegram, email) → find-us map +
address + hours → optional friction-killer FAQ accordion → low-emphasis
online-coaching link → footer. Whiteboard energy, heavy Cyrillic-capable
display type, warm and direct. AA contrast on the amber CTA. Show
Ukrainian and English. Mobile: form first and full-width with reassurance
below it, contact channels as stacked one-tap actions (which may convert
better than the form — give them prominence), map full-width with
tap-to-directions.
