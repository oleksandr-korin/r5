## Purpose & goal

Capture **remote** clients who can't train at the Dnipro box but want
Dmytro's programming and his HRV/recovery method. This is the **English
wedge** and the differentiator play: "a still-competing champion's method,
wherever you are." Different audience, different conversion from the rest
of the site.

**Primary CTA:** *Apply / enquire about online coaching.*
(Note: on *this* page only, the online CTA is primary; the local "book a
session" CTA steps back.)

---

## Desktop structure (top → bottom)

1. **Hero (dark).** Heavy headline aimed at the remote athlete (e.g.
   *"Train with Dmytro from anywhere."*). Sub-line: his method, built on
   25 years and a national-champion engine, delivered remotely. Primary
   CTA (apply/enquire). The dark treatment signals this is a distinct,
   premium track.

2. **The method (HRV) — the centrepiece.** This page is where the HRV /
   nervous-system-recovery approach gets its fullest, clearest
   explanation, because it's the reason to choose him over a generic
   online programmer. Plain language: train hard, recover by the numbers,
   build strength that lasts. Visual: "training-manual" panel.

3. **Why Dmytro (credibility, condensed).** A tight proof row pulling the
   strongest verified hits — #1 Masters 40+ Ukraine 2026, 25 years
   coaching, Master of Sport, CrossFit L2 + Gymnastics, HRV-certified,
   builds champions. Stat/credential blocks. Links to the full Coach page.

4. **How online coaching works.** A clear 3–4 step "how it works" strip:
   *apply → assessment/consult → personalized programming + HRV guidance →
   ongoing check-ins.* Make the intangible concrete. (Confirm real
   process with Dmytro.)

5. **Who it's for.** Short qualifier block — masters athletes, busy
   professionals, people who keep getting injured/burned out, anyone who
   wants longevity not just intensity. Helps the right people self-select.

6. **Plans / pricing.** Option cards for the remote offer (e.g. tiers by
   touchpoint frequency). **Placeholder — confirm with Dmytro;** if not
   set, use an "enquire for details" card instead of fake prices.

7. **FAQ.** A few honest questions: equipment needed, how HRV is measured,
   languages (UA/EN), time zones, commitment. Reduces friction for a
   higher-consideration purchase.

8. **CTA band.** *Apply for online coaching.* (Primary here.) A small
   secondary line for locals: "In Dnipro? Train in the box →."

9. **Footer.** Global.

## Mobile adaptation (~390)

- **Hero:** full-width dark, heavy headline reflows, single primary CTA;
  sticky CTA on scroll.
- **Method (2):** full-width manual panel; keep the plain-language
  explanation, trim length.
- **Credibility row (3):** 2×N stat-block grid.
- **How it works (4):** vertical numbered steps.
- **Pricing (6):** stacked option cards; flag a recommended tier.
- **FAQ (7):** accordion.
- Touch targets 44px+, CTA 56px.
